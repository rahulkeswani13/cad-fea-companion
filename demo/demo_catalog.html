🚁 Mission Briefing: Designing a High-Performance Quadcopter UAV Arm
Real-World Engineering Arc

Walk through the end-to-end design story from the perspective of an aerospace structural simulation engineer. The goal: Design a 180 mm quadcopter arm, cut mass by ~17% via generative strut-lattice pocketing while preserving load-bearing chord rails, ensure structural integrity under a 120 N motor thrust, and verify mesh independence.

PHASE 01
Boundary Constraints

Establish root clamp boss for chassis bolting and tip motor ring interface under 120 N lift thrust.

PHASE 02
Topology & Lattice

Convert solid box core into an exposed X-truss lattice with 1.5 mm chord rails to achieve ~−17% mass savings.

PHASE 03
Simulation Honesty

Cross-check 3D CalculiX FEA against closed-form analytical cantilever beam equations to guard against mesh divergence.

PHASE 04
Mesh Convergence

Execute multi-density Gmsh sweeps (5.0 → 3.5 → 2.5 mm) to guarantee asymptotic stress convergence.

PHASE 05
Material Trade Study

Evaluate Al 6061-T6 vs Al 7075-T6 vs Ti-6Al-4V under strict safety factor threshold (≥ 1.5).
